# AIMarket developer & investor portal — product brief

## Purpose

Explain the potential value of AIMarket to two audiences without presenting hypotheses as shipped products:

- developers should understand what can be built, which rails already exist, and where their product layer begins;
- investors should understand how the technical system creates economic leverage across multiple verticals.

The portal is an evidence-backed map of the system and its opportunity space. It is not a conventional activation funnel, a complete product catalog, or a securities prospectus.

## Core narrative

`declared source → signed relay envelope → provenance/freshness checks → paid invoke → policy-gated settlement → vertical product`

Every major section should reinforce the connection between a technical property and an economic consequence.

A signature proves attribution and integrity of the signed envelope. It does not independently prove that an upstream physical-world claim is correct. Public copy must distinguish raw sensing, third-party relays, curated registries, derived context, and forecasts.

## Information architecture

1. Hero: category thesis and two audience entrances.
2. Audience paths: developer and investor outcomes.
3. System thesis: six-stage value chain and technical-to-economic mapping.
4. Practical paths: see, buy, publish, build, invest.
5. Live rails and demonstrable use cases.
6. Opportunity map: a catalog of potential product ideas grouped by system direction.
7. Sensor supply path.
8. Investor narrative, adjacent markets, and sources.

## Opportunity-map maturity model

- **Live surface** — a product or callable surface is operating now.
- **Build-ready** — the shared rails exist; a focused application layer remains to be built.
- **Design partner** — the path is credible but requires domain validation with a partner.
- **Hypothesis** — a possible application that still needs demand and feasibility evidence.

Statuses describe evidence maturity, not market size or strategic importance.

## Acceptance criteria

- The first screen offers explicit routes for developers and investors.
- The system is understandable as one value chain before internal product names dominate.
- The opportunity map is explicitly described as potential products, not a catalog of shipped products.
- Every maturity status has a plain-language definition.
- Existing ideas and practical five-path onboarding remain accessible.
- All new public copy works in EN, RU, ES, FR, and ZH.
- Static validation passes and the page remains usable on desktop and mobile.
